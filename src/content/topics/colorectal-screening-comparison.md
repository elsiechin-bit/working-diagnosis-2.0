      ### Where they actually differ
  **Start age:** NZ (60 - 74) is the oldest-starting programme; AU and UK have both expanded toward 45 - 50; US guidelines moved to 45 in 2021 (ACS) and 2022 (USPSTF).
  **Modality:** NZ and AU use FIT exclusively. UK uses FIT, removed the flexible sigmoidoscopy strand (post-COVID). US guidelines offer the most modality choice (FIT, colonoscopy, CT colonography, stool DNA).
  **Interval:** FIT every 2 years is the standard in NZ/AU/UK; US guidelines also allow annual FIT and 10-yearly colonoscopy.
