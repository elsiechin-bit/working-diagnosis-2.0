      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **DOACs are now first-line** everywhere; warfarin is reserved for antiphospholipid syndrome and severe renal impairment, and LMWH for pregnancy.
        **Duration:** 3 months for provoked VTE; unprovoked VTE is increasingly managed with extended reduced-dose DOAC, weighed against bleeding risk.
        **Cancer-associated VTE:** DOACs are increasingly preferred, with LMWH favoured where luminal GI bleeding risk is high.
