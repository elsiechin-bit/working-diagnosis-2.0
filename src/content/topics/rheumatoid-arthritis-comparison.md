      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **Access gates escalation:** NICE’s DAS28 over 5.1 rule and Pharmac/PBS criteria shape when biologics start, more than clinician judgement alone.
        **JAK caution:** EULAR foregrounds cardiovascular and malignancy risk when choosing JAK inhibitors.
        **Shared ground:** early methotrexate, treat-to-target, and steroids only as a short bridge are universal.
