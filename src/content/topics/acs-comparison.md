      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **Reperfusion thresholds:** all favour primary PCI, but the 120-minute rule and pharmaco-invasive fallback dominate practice across NZ’s regional geography.
        **P2Y12 choice:** ESC prefers prasugrel over ticagrelor for PCI; NICE leads with ticagrelor.
        **LDL targets:** ESC sets an explicit under-1.4 mmol/L goal, while others emphasise high-intensity statin without a single number.
