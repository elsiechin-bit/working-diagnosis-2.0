      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **Biologic access:** the menu is similar, but Pharmac (NZ) and PBS (AU) criteria shape first-choice biologic more than clinical preference.
        **Crohn’s induction:** exclusive enteral nutrition is first-line in children everywhere; 5-ASA has no role in Crohn’s.
        **Treat-to-target:** calprotectin-guided monitoring and STRIDE-II endpoints are now common ground.
