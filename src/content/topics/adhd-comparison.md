      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **Who prescribes:** the sharpest divergence - NZ has recently widened GP prescribing, where elsewhere shared care follows specialist initiation.
        **Stimulants first:** all four agree stimulants are first-line, with atomoxetine the main non-stimulant alternative.
        **Controlled-drug rules:** authority and approval requirements differ substantially by country and state, shaping access.
