## Overview


**Medication Calculators** → Overseas → NZ
      Overseas → *NZ* Medicines
      For patients arriving from overseas wanting to continue their medicines - find the New Zealand generic name, local brand, drug class, equivalent dosing and what to watch when switching.

      ⚠︎
      **Overseas prescriptions cannot be dispensed in New Zealand** - the patient needs a new NZ prescription. Use this as a starting point, confirm the exact product and dose against the NZF, and check funding/Special Authority before prescribing.
