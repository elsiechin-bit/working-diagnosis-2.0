      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **NICE is the outlier:** NG193 advises against paracetamol, NSAIDs, opioids and gabapentinoids for chronic primary pain, recommending exercise, psychological therapy and an antidepressant instead.
        **Opioids:** all four discourage them in chronic non-cancer pain and emphasise deprescribing.
        **Neuropathic pain:** the same four first-line agents (amitriptyline, duloxetine, gabapentin, pregabalin) are shared across guidelines.
