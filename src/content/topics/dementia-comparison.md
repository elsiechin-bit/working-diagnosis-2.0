      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **Antipsychotics:** every guideline restricts them to severe distress or risk, citing stroke and mortality harm, with non-drug care first.
        **Cognitive tools:** NZ and AU foreground culturally fair instruments (MoCA, RUDAS); NICE uses brief screens like the 6-CIT.
        **Shared ground:** cholinesterase inhibitors for mild-to-moderate Alzheimer’s, memantine for moderate-to-severe disease, and carer support throughout.
