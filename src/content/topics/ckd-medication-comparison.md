      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **SGLT2 expansion:** all now back SGLT2 inhibitors for albuminuric CKD, but eGFR floors and funding criteria differ.
        **BP target:** KDIGO pushes systolic under 120, more aggressive than the 130/80 used elsewhere.
        **Finerenone:** recommended for diabetic CKD internationally, but access varies in NZ and AU.
