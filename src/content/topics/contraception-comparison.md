      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **Access is the differentiator:** NZ has expanded free LARC and the UK offers an over-the-counter POP - the clinical advice is otherwise closely aligned.
        **LARC-first:** all four promote long-acting reversible contraception as the most effective, offered first.
        **Shared frameworks:** UKMEC and WHO MEC underpin eligibility everywhere, so safety decisions are highly consistent.
