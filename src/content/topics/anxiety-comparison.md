      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **Stepped care:** NICE formalises a low-to-high intensity sequence; others recommend the same tools less prescriptively.
        **First drug:** NICE names sertraline specifically, while NZ/AU keep an open SSRI choice.
        **Shared ground:** CBT and SSRIs first-line, benzodiazepines restricted, treatment for at least a year - consistent everywhere.
