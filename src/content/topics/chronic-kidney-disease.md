#### Confirm CKD (two readings >3 months apart)
      
        - eGFR <60 mL/min/1.73m² on two occasions ≥3 months apart, OR
        - Urine ACR ≥3 mg/mmol on two occasions ≥3 months apart, OR
        - Structural kidney abnormality (polycystic kidney, transplant)
        - A single low eGFR may represent AKI — confirm with repeat testing
      
      #### Urine ACR classification
      
        **A1 (<3 mg/mmol):** normal to mildly increased
        **A2 (3–30 mg/mmol):** moderately increased
        **A3 (>30 mg/mmol):** severely increased — aggressive treatment indicated
        - Early morning spot urine preferred; if contaminated (UTI, menstruation) repeat
      
      #### Investigations to identify cause and complications
      
        - Urine: ACR, microscopy (haematuria, casts), MCS
        - Blood: UEC, eGFR, FBC, glucose/HbA1c, lipids, calcium, phosphate, PTH (G4+), bicarbonate
        - Renal ultrasound: size, echogenicity, obstruction, polycystic disease
        - Consider: ANA, ANCA, anti-GBM, complement, immunoglobulins, serum protein electrophoresis — if rapidly progressive, haematuria, or young patient

    🛡️Slowing progression▾
    
      #### RAAS blockade — cornerstone of CKD management
      
        **ACE inhibitor or ARB** for all CKD with ACR >30 mg/mmol (A3) — regardless of BP
        - Also for ACR 3–30 (A2) if diabetic or hypertensive
        - Target BP <130/80 mmHg in CKD (lower if significant proteinuria)
        - Expect 10–20% rise in creatinine and small rise in K⁺ on initiation — acceptable and expected; stop if >30% creatinine rise or K⁺ >6.0 mmol/L
        - Do NOT combine ACEi + ARB — increases AKI and hyperkalaemia risk without additional benefit
      
      #### SGLT2 inhibitors — now standard of care
      
        **Dapagliflozin 10 mg** (DAPA-CKD trial) and **empagliflozin 10 mg** (EMPA-KIDNEY) reduce CKD progression and ESRD by ~40%
        - Benefit in diabetic AND non-diabetic CKD (including IgA nephropathy)
        - Use down to eGFR ≥20 for kidney/CV protection; glycaemic effect diminishes below eGFR 45
        - Hold during acute illness or hospitalisation
      
      #### Finerenone (non-steroidal MRA)
      
        - Add-on to ACEi/ARB in diabetic CKD with ACR >30 and eGFR ≥25
        - Reduces CKD progression and CV events (FIDELIO-DKD, FIGARO-DKD)
        - Less hyperkalaemia than spironolactone; monitor K⁺

    ⚕️Managing CKD complications▾
    
      #### Anaemia (G3b+)
      
        - Normochromic, normocytic — reduced erythropoietin production
        - Check iron first — correct iron deficiency (IV iron preferred when eGFR <30)
        - Erythropoiesis-stimulating agents (ESAs): epoetin, darbepoetin — specialist decision; target Hb 100–120 g/L
      
      #### Mineral metabolism (G3b+)
      
        - Hyperphosphataemia: dietary phosphate restriction; phosphate binders (calcium carbonate, sevelamer)
        - Vitamin D deficiency: ergocalciferol or cholecalciferol for 25-OH deficiency; active vitamin D (calcitriol/alfacalcidol) for secondary hyperparathyroidism when PTH elevated
        - Hypercalcaemia: reduce calcium-containing phosphate binders
      
      #### Metabolic acidosis
      
        - Target serum bicarbonate ≥22 mmol/L
        - Sodium bicarbonate 500 mg–1 g BD–TDS
        - Slows CKD progression and reduces muscle wasting
      
      #### Hyperkalaemia
      
        - Review medications: ACEi/ARB, MRA, NSAIDS, trimethoprim
        - Dietary potassium restriction (avoid high-K⁺ foods: bananas, potatoes, tomatoes)
        - Patiromer or sodium zirconium cyclosilicate (SZC) — potassium binders; allow ACEi/ARB continuation

      #### Referral criteria to nephrology
      
        - eGFR <30 (G4+) for dialysis preparation planning
        - Rapidly progressive CKD (>5 mL/min/1.73m²/year decline)
        - ACR >70 mg/mmol despite optimal therapy
        - Unexplained haematuria with proteinuria (consider glomerulonephritis)
        - Refractory hypertension, hyperkalaemia or anaemia
        - Suspected rare cause: ANCA vasculitis, anti-GBM, myeloma
