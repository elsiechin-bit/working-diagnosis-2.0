      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **First-line split:** NZ keeps trimethoprim prominent, while NICE and IDSA lead with nitrofurantoin amid trimethoprim resistance.
        **Short courses:** all favour short courses - trimethoprim ~3 days, nitrofurantoin ~5 days - in uncomplicated cystitis.
        **Selective testing:** dipstick and culture are reserved for atypical, recurrent or pregnant cases rather than routine use.
