      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **HPV doses:** NZ, AU and UK now use a single adolescent dose, while US ACIP retains two doses at 11 to 12 years.
        **Influenza funding:** the US recommends universal annual vaccination from 6 months, whereas NZ/AU/UK fund by age and risk.
        **Timing varies, principles agree:** the same core antigens are delivered, but funded ages and dose schedules are set nationally.
