#### Symptoms
          
            - Dysmenorrhoea: severe, progressive pelvic pain during menstruation
            - Dyspareunia: pain during or after sexual intercourse
            - Dyschezia: painful bowel movements
            - Infertility: 30–50% of women with endometriosis are infertile

          Management Approach
          ^

          #### First-Line Treatment
          
            - NSAIDs for pain management
            - Combined hormonal contraceptives: extended or continuous regimens
            - Progestin-only methods: medroxyprogesterone acetate, levonorgestrel IUD
          
          #### Second-Line and Specialist Options
          
            - GnRH agonists or antagonists
            - Surgical excision or ablation
            - Assisted reproduction for infertility
