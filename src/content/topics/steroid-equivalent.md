          Equivalents compare **anti-inflammatory (glucocorticoid) potency only** - they do not reflect mineralocorticoid needs or replacement dosing in adrenal insufficiency.
          **Dexamethasone and betamethasone** are long-acting with negligible mineralocorticoid effect - useful where fluid retention matters, but with greater HPA-axis suppression.
          **Hydrocortisone and cortisone** carry significant mineralocorticoid activity (relevant in replacement, not anti-inflammatory use).
          **Taper** any course longer than about 3 weeks, or repeated courses, to avoid adrenal suppression; consider bone, glucose and gastric protection for prolonged use.
