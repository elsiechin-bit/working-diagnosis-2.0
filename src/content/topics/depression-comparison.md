      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **Less-severe depression:** NICE actively steers away from routine antidepressants first-line, offering a menu where NZ/AU are more medication-permissive.
        **Discontinuation:** NICE 2022 reframed tapering as a slow, sometimes months-long process to limit withdrawal.
        **Access gap:** the therapies are agreed - the difference is delivery, with Australia (Better Access) and England (Talking Therapies) more structured than NZ.
