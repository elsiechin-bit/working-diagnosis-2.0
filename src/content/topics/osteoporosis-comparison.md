      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **Different tools:** NZ/AU lean on Garvan, the UK on QFracture/NOGG and the world on FRAX - the same patient can land at different thresholds.
        **Denosumab rebound:** every guideline now warns that denosumab must not be stopped without follow-on therapy.
        **Funding gates access:** NZ PHARMAC criteria shape who reaches denosumab and teriparatide more than risk score alone.
