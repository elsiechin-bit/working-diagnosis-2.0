#### Overview
          Autoimmune inflammatory arthritis; T-cell and B-cell mediated destruction of synovial joints. Affects 0.5–1% of population; female predominance 3:1.

          #### Diagnostic criteria (ACR/EULAR 2010)
          
            - Joint involvement (score 0–5).
            - Serology: RF or anti-CCP (score 0–3).
            - Acute phase reactants: ESR or CRP (score 0–1).
            - Duration ≥6 weeks.
            - Total score ≥6 indicates definite RA.
          
          #### Key serological markers
          
            **Rheumatoid factor (RF):** Present in 70–80%; can predate symptoms by years.
            **Anti-CCP (cyclic citrullinated peptide):** Highly specific (95%); better predictor of joint damage; present earlier than RF.

        🎯Treatment targets and remission▾
        
          #### Treat-to-target strategy
          
            - Goal: Remission or low disease activity within 3 months; if not achieved, escalate therapy.
            - Prevents joint damage and functional decline.
          
          #### Remission definitions
          
            **Boolean:** TJC ≤1, SJC ≤1, CRP ≤1 mg/dL, PGA ≤1 on 0–10 scale.
            **CDAI:** Clinical Disease Activity Index ≤2.8.
            **DAS28:** Disease Activity Score 

        💊Pharmacological management▾
        
          #### Conventional DMARDs
          
            **Methotrexate:** First-line; 15–25 mg weekly (oral or SC); response in 6–8 weeks. Monitor CBC, LFTs, creatinine (renal impairment increases toxicity). Folic acid supplementation essential.
            **Sulfasalazine, hydroxychloroquine:** Slower onset; often used in combination.
            **Leflunomide:** Alternative to methotrexate; monitor LFTs and CBC.
          
          #### Biologic DMARDs
          
            **TNF inhibitors (infliximab, adalimumab, etanercept):** Reduce inflammatory cytokine; high efficacy. Screen for TB before starting. Increased infection risk.
            **IL-6 inhibitors (tocilizumab):** Monoclonal anti-IL-6; effective if TNF inhibitors fail.
            **B-cell depleting (rituximab):** For anti-CCP or RF positive; can be combined with methotrexate.
            **JAK inhibitors (baricitinib, upadacitinib):** Oral; target intracellular signalling; emerging role, oral advantage, but higher infection/CV event risk in some.
          
          #### Combination therapy
          
            - Methotrexate + biologic is standard for moderate-severe RA.

      📚Key references▾

          #### Key guidelines & evidence
          
            - EULAR 2021 recommendations for RA management.
            - ACR 2021 Rheumatoid Arthritis Classification Criteria.
            - Treat-to-target strategy — aim for remission or low disease activity.
            - DMARDs (methotrexate, sulfasalazine, leflunomide) are foundation therapy.
            - TNF-inhibitors, IL-6 inhibitors, JAK inhibitors — escalate if inadequate response to conventional DMARD.
            - ATTRACT trial — TNF-inhibitor + methotrexate superior to methotrexate alone.
            - NZ Rheumatology Society RA resources.
