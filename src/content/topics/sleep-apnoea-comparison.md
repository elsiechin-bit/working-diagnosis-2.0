      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **Testing pathway:** home sleep testing is increasingly first-line, but the UK leans on respiratory polygraphy and reserves full PSG for unclear cases.
        **Driving rules:** DVLA notification is mandatory in the UK; NZ and AU apply NZTA/Austroads standards with clinician judgement.
        **Shared ground:** CPAP for moderate-severe disease, mandibular devices for milder or CPAP-intolerant patients, weight loss for all.
