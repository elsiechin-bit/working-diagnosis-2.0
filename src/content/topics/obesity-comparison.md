      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **Funding is the divide:** the GLP-1 evidence is shared, but NZ funds almost none of it while the UK funds within specialist tiers.
        **Ethnic thresholds:** NZ and international Asian cut-points lower the BMI at which action is taken - central to equity.
        **Beyond BMI:** guidance is shifting toward complication-centred assessment rather than BMI alone.
