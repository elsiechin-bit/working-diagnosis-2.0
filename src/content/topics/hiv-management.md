#### Pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP)
          
            **Indications:** MSM, transgender, sex workers, serodiscordant couples, PWID, prison populations, or individuals with ongoing risk behaviour.
            **Regimen:** TDF/FTC (tenofovir/emtricitabine) daily, or event-driven dosing for intermittent use.
            **Efficacy:** 99% in adherent users; drops to ~50% if inconsistent use.
            **Monitoring:** Quarterly HIV testing, 6-monthly renal function (TDF can affect kidney function), annual STI screening.
            **NZ access:** Not funded on primary care; available privately (~NZ$150–300/month) or through some sexual health clinics.
          
          #### Post-exposure prophylaxis (PEP)
          
            **Indication:** Recent (≤72 hours) potential exposure (needlestick, sexual exposure, occupational).
            **Regimen:** 3-drug ART (e.g. TDF/FTC + raltegravir or dolutegravir); 28 days.
            **Efficacy:** ~81% if started within 2 hours; drops to ~13% if delayed >72 hours.
            **Available 24/7:** ED/sexual health clinic; emergency contact for after-hours.

        🔬Diagnosis and monitoring▾
        
          #### Testing
          
            **4th generation test:** Detects HIV p24 antigen and antibody; window period ~18–45 days post-exposure.
            **Confirmatory:** Western blot or HIV-1/2 differentiation assay if 4th gen positive.
            **Baseline CD4 and VL:** Essential for prognosis and ART initiation decision.

        💊Antiretroviral therapy (ART)▾
        
          #### When to start
          
            - Immediately after diagnosis (within weeks), regardless of CD4 count.
            - Adherence support critical; emphasise U=U benefits and prevention of resistance.
          
          #### First-line regimens (DHHS, WHO, NZ guidelines)
          
            **Integrase inhibitors + 2 NRTIs:** (DTG or BIC or EVG/c + TDF/FTC or TAF/FTC) preferred; fewer drug interactions, better barrier to resistance.
            **NNRTI-based:** (Efavirenz or rilpivirine + 2 NRTIs); older but still effective.
            **PI-based:** (Atazanavir/ritonavir or darunavir/ritonavir + 2 NRTIs); if resistance or specific situations.
          
          #### Monitoring
          
            - CD4 and VL at baseline, 4–8 weeks, then 3–6 monthly until undetectable (CD4 >200), then annually.
            - Adherence support, side effect management, drug interaction monitoring.
          
          #### Undetectable equals untransmittable (U=U)
          
            VL 6 months prevents sexual transmission (PARTNER and PARTNER2 studies).
            - Critical public health message; reduces stigma and supports prevention.

        🏥Life expectancy and long-term management▾
        
          #### Outcomes with modern ART
          
            - Life expectancy near-normal for those starting ART with CD4 >200.
            - Mortality from non-AIDS causes (cardiovascular, hepatic, renal, cancer) now exceeds AIDS-related deaths.
          
          #### Chronic management
          
            - Annual STI screening; HPV vaccination if CD4 >200.
            - CV risk assessment; smoking cessation; lipid and glucose monitoring.
            - Mental health support; drug/alcohol counselling; adherence support ongoing.
            - HBV and HCV coinfection; manage accordingly.
