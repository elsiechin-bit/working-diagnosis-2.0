## Prognosis


The traditional narrative-schizophrenia as a relentless, progressively deteriorating illness-is wrong. Roughly one-third of people have a single psychotic episode and remit fully. One-third have multiple episodes but achieve functional recovery between them. One-third have persistent symptoms and functional impairment. Outcomes are better in women, in those with good premorbid function, in those who engage with treatment early, and in societies with less stigma and better social support.

        The term "recovery" in schizophrenia now encompasses not just symptom remission but also social inclusion, meaningful activity, stable housing and relationships. These often matter more to patients than the absence of voices.
