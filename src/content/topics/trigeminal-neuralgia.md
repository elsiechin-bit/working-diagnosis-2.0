#### Trigeminal neuralgiaSudden, severe, shooting facial pain in CN V distribution; unilateral >90%.
#### Carbamazepine- Gold standard; 80% efficacy; start 100 mg daily, titrate to 400-1200 mg/day.- Side effects: dizziness, diplopia, rash (including SJS).
