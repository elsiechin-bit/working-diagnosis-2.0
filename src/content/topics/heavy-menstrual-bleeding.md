#### History
          
            - Bleeding pattern, flooding, clots, cycle regularity, and impact on life; intermenstrual or postcoital bleeding
            Bleeding since menarche or a personal/family bleeding history suggests a **coagulopathy** (e.g. von Willebrand disease)
            - Symptoms of anaemia; contraceptive and fertility wishes; cervical screening status
          
          #### Examination & tests
          
            - Abdominal and pelvic examination; pregnancy test where relevant
            **FBC and ferritin** for everyone; coagulation screen if history suggests; TSH only if thyroid symptoms
            **Transvaginal ultrasound** is first-line imaging for structural causes
          
          #### Endometrial sampling
          
            Biopsy if aged **45 or over**, intermenstrual or postcoital bleeding, risk factors for endometrial hyperplasia/cancer (obesity, PCOS, unopposed oestrogen), or treatment failure
          
          Red flags - investigate / referPostmenopausal bleeding, persistent intermenstrual or postcoital bleeding, a suspicious cervix, or age 45+ with risk factors need prompt assessment for endometrial or cervical pathology.

        💊Medical management▾
        
          #### First-line
          
            **Levonorgestrel intrauterine system (LNG-IUS)** - most effective medical option; also provides contraception. Counsel about irregular bleeding in the first months.
          
          #### Non-hormonal (good if contraception not wanted, taken during periods)
          
            **Tranexamic acid** - reduces loss by around a third
            **NSAIDs** (e.g. mefenamic acid) - reduce loss and help dysmenorrhoea
          
          #### Other hormonal
          
            **Combined oral contraceptive** or cyclical/long-course **progestogens**
            **GnRH analogues** short-term to shrink fibroids before surgery

        🩺Procedural & surgical options▾

            **Endometrial ablation** - for completed families, no significant structural cause
            **Myomectomy** or **uterine artery embolisation** - for fibroids, preserving the uterus
            **Hysterectomy** - definitive, when other options fail or are declined and family is complete

      Don't forget the anaemiaHeavy menstrual bleeding is a leading cause of iron deficiency. Check ferritin, replace iron, and recheck - treating the bleeding and the iron deficiency together.
