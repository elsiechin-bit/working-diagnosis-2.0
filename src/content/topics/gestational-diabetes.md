## Overview


GDM is glucose intolerance first recognised in pregnancy. It matters because hyperglycaemia drives macrosomia, shoulder dystocia, neonatal hypoglycaemia, and longer-term metabolic risk for both mother and child.

   **NZ practice:** HbA1c at booking (to detect pre-existing diabetes), then a **75g OGTT at 24-28 weeks**. The polycose/glucose challenge is used in some pathways.
   **Earlier testing** if high risk: previous GDM, raised booking HbA1c, BMI >30, family history, ethnicity (South Asian, Māori, Pacific), previous macrosomia, PCOS.
   - Diagnosis uses fasting and post-load thresholds (verify the current local OGTT cut-offs).

      🍽Management - the stepped approach▾
      
   **Education and glucose self-monitoring** (fasting and post-prandial targets).
   **Diet and physical activity first** - controls the majority of GDM.
   **Metformin** if targets are not met (acceptable and widely used in pregnancy).
   **Insulin** when oral therapy is insufficient or for significant hyperglycaemia - the most effective and titratable option.
   **Fetal surveillance** (growth scans) and planning of timing/mode of birth in the diabetic antenatal service.
  
  Target-driven escalationDon’t linger on diet alone if glucose targets are repeatedly missed - timely metformin/insulin protects the baby.

      👶Birth & postnatal▾
      
   **Neonatal hypoglycaemia** risk - babies need glucose monitoring and early feeding.
   **Stop GDM medications** after delivery; glucose usually normalises immediately.
   **Postnatal OGTT or HbA1c** (around 6-12 weeks) and then **annual diabetes screening** - up to half develop type 2 diabetes within a decade.
   - Advise on weight, activity and the markedly raised recurrence risk in future pregnancies; encourage breastfeeding.

   ✦**Check booking HbA1c** to catch pre-existing (undiagnosed) type 2 diabetes.
   ✦**Metformin and insulin are both safe** and appropriate in pregnancy.
   ✦**The postnatal test gets missed** - build it into the plan before discharge.
   ✦**GDM is a window** into lifelong cardiometabolic risk - use it.
