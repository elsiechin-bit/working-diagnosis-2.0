#### ACR Criteria
          Widespread pain lasting more than 3 months, combined with symptoms of fatigue, sleep disturbance, and mood changes.

          #### Examination Findings
          
            - Normal laboratory work and imaging
            - Tender points or generalized allodynia
            - No evidence of alternative diagnosis

          Management Approach
          ^

          #### Multimodal Treatment
          
            - Patient education and normalisation
            - Regular aerobic and strengthening exercise
            - Cognitive behavioural therapy
            - Pharmacotherapy: pregabalin, duloxetine, amitriptyline
