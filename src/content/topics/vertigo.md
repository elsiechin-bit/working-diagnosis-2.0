#### True vertigoIllusion of movement (room spinning); suggests vestibular dysfunction.
#### Peripheral causes (80%)- BPPV, vestibular neuritis, Meniere disease, vestibular migraine.#### BPPV treatment- Canalith repositioning (Epley manoeuvre); 80-90% cure with 1-3 sessions.

      📚Key references▾
      
        #### Key guidelines- AAO-HNS Vertigo and Dizziness Guidelines.- BPPV — Epley manoeuvre highly effective; 90% resolve within 1-2 weeks.- Vestibular neuritis — corticosteroids may reduce recovery time.- Vestibular migraine managed with migraine preventives.
