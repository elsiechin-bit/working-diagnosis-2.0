      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **Subclinical thresholds:** all treat TSH >10, but the threshold for treating mild (4 - 10) elevation - especially in older adults - differs.
        **T4/T3 combination:** remains contested: NICE discourages it, while BTA/ATA permit a cautious specialist-led trial.
        **Monotherapy holds:** levothyroxine titrated to a normal TSH is the shared standard everywhere.
