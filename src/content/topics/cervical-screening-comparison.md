      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **HPV primary, different timing:** Australia led the switch (2017), NZ followed (2023) and the UK is HPV-primary - all replacing cytology as the first test.
        **Self-collection & equity:** NZ and Australia offer self-collection to everyone eligible - a deliberate lever to close equity gaps for under-screened groups.
        **Longer intervals:** HPV-negative results now permit ~5-year recall, with England moving from 3 to 5 years.
