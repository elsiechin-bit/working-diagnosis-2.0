      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **Comorbidity-first:** every guideline now picks the second drug by heart, kidney and vascular disease rather than by HbA1c alone.
        **Funding shapes practice:** the clinical advice converges, but NZ Special Authority rules for dulaglutide and empagliflozin decide who actually gets them.
        **Equity:** NZ is distinctive in placing Māori and Pacific equity at the centre of screening and funded access.
