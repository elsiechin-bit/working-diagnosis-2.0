      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **Risk score:** Australia and the ESC have dropped the sex category - they now use CHA₂DS₂-VA. NZ, the UK and AHA/ACC retain CHA₂DS₂-VASc.
        **Drug access:** The clinical advice is near-identical (DOAC over warfarin), but NZ funding shapes practice - dabigatran and rivaroxaban are the funded options, where apixaban is first-choice elsewhere.
        **Rhythm control:** The ESC 2024 position is the most assertive - early rhythm control and first-line ablation for selected patients; NZ practice is more access-limited.
