## Overview


New Zealand’s cessation framework is deliberately brief so it gets done. Every patient contact is an opportunity.

   **A - Ask** and document smoking status.
   **B - give Brief advice** to stop, regardless of readiness. Even 30 seconds measurably increases quitting.
   **C - offer Cessation support**: a combination of behavioural support and pharmacotherapy, or referral (Quitline, local stop-smoking service).
  
  Brief advice worksThe number needed to treat for simple GP advice is high but the intervention is so cheap and the disease burden so large that it is one of the most cost-effective things in all of medicine.

      💊Pharmacotherapy▾
      
   #### Combination NRT**How:** a long-acting patch for baseline plus a short-acting form (gum, lozenge, inhalator, mouth spray) for breakthrough cravings**Why combination:** clearly better than single-form NRT**Safe** in cardiovascular disease and pregnancy (intermittent forms preferred in pregnancy)
   #### Varenicline**What:** α4β2 nicotinic partial agonist - the most effective single agent**Start:** 1 week before quit date; titrate over first week**SE:** nausea, vivid dreams; monitor mood
   #### Nortriptyline / bupropionSecond-line options where first-line is unsuitable or declined
   #### Vaping (nicotine)An effective quit aid for some; far less harmful than smoking but not harmless. Pragmatic for smokers who have not succeeded with approved therapies; aim to eventually stop vaping too.

      🤝Behavioural support & follow-up▾
      
   Set a **quit date** and start pharmacotherapy around it (varenicline before; NRT on the day).
   Refer to **Quitline / local services** - structured behavioural support independently improves success.
   **Follow up early** (first week or two) and repeatedly - relapse is part of the process, not failure.
   Address **triggers, routines and withdrawal**; treat lapses as learning, and re-engage.
   - Most successful quitters make several attempts - normalise this and keep offering support.

   ✦**Ask everyone, every time.** Status changes and so does readiness.
   ✦**Combine** a patch with a fast-acting NRT - don’t rely on one form.
   ✦**Relapse is expected.** Re-treat without judgment.
   ✦**Smoking changes drug metabolism** (CYP1A2) - clozapine, olanzapine and theophylline levels rise on quitting.
