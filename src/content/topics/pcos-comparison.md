      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **Letrozole-first:** all now favour letrozole over clomiphene for ovulation induction, reflecting the modern evidence base.
        **Diagnosis:** the international 2023 guideline accepts AMH in place of ultrasound for adults; others still anchor on Rotterdam ultrasound.
        **Shared ground:** lifestyle first, COC for menstrual/androgenic features, OGTT-based metabolic screening.
