          **Methadone and buprenorphine** are deliberately excluded - their conversions are non-linear and dose-dependent. Seek specialist or palliative-care advice.
          **Fentanyl & buprenorphine patches** reach steady state slowly; account for the depot when switching and provide breakthrough cover.
          **Renal impairment:** morphine and codeine accumulate active metabolites - prefer oxycodone or fentanyl and reduce doses.
          **Breakthrough dose** is usually about **1/10th to 1/6th** of the total daily oral morphine equivalent, as an immediate-release opioid.
