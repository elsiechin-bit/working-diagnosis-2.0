#### Rome IV functional constipation
          
            ≥2 of: straining >25% of defecations, lumpy/hard stools, sensation of incomplete evacuation, sensation of anorectal obstruction, manual manoeuvres, 
            - Symptoms ≥3 months, onset ≥6 months prior.
          
          #### Subtypes
          
            **Normal transit:** Slow colonic transit on marker study.
            **Slow transit:** Severe prolonged transit; often female, starts young; risk factors: low fibre, low activity.
            **Outlet/defecatory disorder:** Difficulty evacuating despite normal transit; pelvic floor dysfunction, anismus.
          
          #### Secondary (organic) causes
          
            - Medications (opioids, anticholinergics, iron).
            - Hypothyroidism, diabetes (autonomic dysfunction), CKD.
            - Structural: strictures, adhesions, colorectal cancer (alarm symptom).

        🍎Non-pharmacological management▾
        
          #### Dietary
          
            **Fibre:** 25–30 g/day; gradual increase (sudden high fibre can worsen bloating); soluble and insoluble.
            **Fluids:** Adequate hydration (minimum 8 glasses/day).
            **Avoid:** Low-fibre processed foods, excess dairy (if lactose intolerant), alcohol.
          
          #### Behavioural
          
            - Regular toileting (especially after meals, when colonic activity high).
            - Physical activity (30 min x5 days/week improves transit).
            - Allow adequate time, avoid straining.
          
          #### Pelvic floor physical therapy
          
            - For outlet dysfunction/pelvic floor dyssynergia; biofeedback effective.

        💊Pharmacotherapy▾
        
          #### Osmotic laxatives (first-line)
          
            **Polyethylene glycol (PEG):** Non-absorbable, safe; 17 g daily mixed in water.
            **Lactulose:** 15–30 mL daily; can cause bloating.
            **Magnesium hydroxide:** Works within hours; caution in renal impairment.
          
          #### Stimulant laxatives
          
            **Senna, bisacodyl:** Increase colonic motor activity; effective but risk of dependence if overused; avoid long-term.
          
          #### Stool softeners
          
            **Docusate:** Minimal efficacy alone; often combined with osmotic agents.
          
          #### Secretagogues (newer agents)
          
            **Linaclotide, lubiprostone:** Enhance intestinal fluid secretion; for chronic idiopathic constipation; modestly effective.

      📚Key references▾
      
        #### Key guidelines- NICE Constipation Management Guidelines.- Rome IV criteria for functional constipation diagnosis.- Osmotic laxatives (polyethylene glycol) first-line; stimulant laxatives for inadequate response.- Stool softeners (docusate) minimal evidence; bulk-forming agents require adequate hydration.
