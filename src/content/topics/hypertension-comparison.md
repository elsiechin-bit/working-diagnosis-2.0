      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **Risk vs number:** NZ treats on absolute CVD risk (PREDICT), not the BP threshold alone - the strongest point of divergence.
        **How low to aim:** ESC 2024 (120–129 systolic) and ACC/AHA (<130/80) push lower targets; NICE holds <140/90, and <150/90 at 80+.
        **How to start:** ESC 2024 begins most patients on a single-pill dual combination, while NICE and NZ usually step up from monotherapy using an age/ethnicity-aware sequence.
