#### Core Symptoms
          
            - Severe fatigue lasting 6 months or more
            - Post-exertional malaise: worsening of symptoms after physical or cognitive exertion
            - Unrefreshing sleep
            - Cognitive difficulties ("brain fog")
            - Autonomic symptoms: orthostatic intolerance, heart rate variability

          Management Strategy
          ^

          #### Principles
          
            - Avoid precipitating post-exertional malaise
            - Manage sleep disturbance and pain
            - Support for mood and anxiety
            - Pacing and activity management
            - Specialist referral for severe cases
