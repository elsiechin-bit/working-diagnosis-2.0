#### DSM-5 Criteria
          ADHD requires evidence of persistent inattention and/or hyperactivity-impulsivity that interferes with functioning or development, is present before age 12, and occurs in multiple settings.

          #### Subtypes
          
            - Predominantly inattentive type
            - Predominantly hyperactive-impulsive type
            - Combined presentation

          Assessment in Primary Care
          ^

          #### Key Steps
          
            - Detailed developmental and school history
            - Current symptom severity and functional impairment
            - Rule out alternative diagnoses and comorbidities
            - Assess for mood, anxiety, and substance use disorders
