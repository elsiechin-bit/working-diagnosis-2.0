## Treatment


If your risk is high enough, medicines can meaningfully lower the chance of a fracture. Here are the main options used in New Zealand.

            🥛
            ### Calcium & vitamin D
            The foundation. Gives your bones raw materials and helps other medicines work. Calcium from food is best; a vitamin D supplement is common for older people.
