      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **Youth protocols:** NZ and UK have paused or restricted puberty blockers and cross-sex hormones in under-16s pending more evidence; Australia and WPATH still permit earlier access with appropriate safeguards.
        **Informed consent:** all require it, but WPATH is explicit on "capacity and voluntariness"; NZ has moved toward dual consent (young person + parent) even in older teens.
        **Monitoring intensity:** UK and RCPCH recommend annual bone-density screening in youth on hormones; NZ/AU less prescriptive.
        **Reversibility:** WPATH emphasizes blockers as fully reversible; UK regulatory caution has made this claim more qualified, citing emerging data on pubertal disruption.
