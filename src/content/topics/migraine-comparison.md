      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **CGRP access:** the science agrees these work for refractory migraine, but NZ funding restrictions limit who receives them.
        **Acute therapy aligns:** triptan plus NSAID, taken early, is the shared first-line everywhere.
        **Overuse & safety:** all flag medication-overuse headache and the need to avoid topiramate/valproate in pregnancy.
