      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **CGM access:** the UK funds CGM for all type 1 diabetes; NZ access is governed by evolving funding criteria.
        **HbA1c target:** NICE aspires to 48 mmol/mol, slightly tighter than the ~53 mmol/mol used elsewhere.
        **Shared ground:** basal-bolus or pump therapy, continuous glucose monitoring, structured education and annual complication screening.
