#### Semaglutide (Ozempic, Wegovy) & tirzepatide (Mounjaro)Prescription · Medsafe approved
              
                Evidence  15-22% sustained weight loss in trials, cardiovascular mortality benefit (SELECT); the strongest evidence base of any weight-management drug to date.
                CatchGenuine drugs, genuine benefit - but shortages drove huge demand toward compounded and grey-market versions with none of this quality assurance.

          ⚠️Compounded & grey-market GLP-1ssame molecule, unverified quality▾

              #### Compounded semaglutide / tirzepatideLegal status varies
              
                WhatMade by compounding pharmacies, legally in some jurisdictions during branded shortages; restricted once shortages are declared resolved.
                HarmsDosing errors and salt-form confusion (semaglutide sodium/acetate salts, not the approved base) have caused reported overdose events. Overseas online sellers add contamination and mislabelling risk on top.

          ⛔Unapproved "research chemical" peptidesno regulator has approved these for human use▾

              #### BPC-157 & TB-500Not approved · online only
              
                ClaimMarketed for tendon, ligament and gut healing - "the healing peptide".
                RealityEvidence is almost entirely animal and in-vitro. No adequately powered human trials establish efficacy or a safety profile at self-administered doses. Vials are unregulated - purity and actual peptide content are unverified.

              #### Growth hormone secretagogues (ipamorelin, CJC-1295, sermorelin-adjacent)Not approved · online only
              
                Claim"Boost your own growth hormone" for muscle gain, fat loss and anti-ageing.
                HarmsSame theoretical concerns as growth hormone itself: fluid retention, insulin resistance, and with sustained overuse, acromegalic change. No prescriber monitoring in typical self-use.

              #### Melanotan IINot approved · online only
              
                ClaimMarketed as a "tanning peptide" and libido enhancer.
                HarmsNausea, spontaneous erections (priapism risk), darkening and changes to naevi with a documented dermatology concern about missed melanoma in users. Injection-site and sourcing risks as with other grey-market peptides.

              #### PT-141 / bremelanotideApproved analogue exists (Vyleesi), narrow indication
              
                ClaimMarketed broadly for libido in both sexes via online sellers.
                RealityThe approved form (Vyleesi) is indicated only for premenopausal women with hypoactive sexual desire disorder, under prescriber supervision, with known nausea and blood-pressure effects. Widespread casual online use goes well beyond this approval.

            Sourcing is the core riskThese products are sold with "not for human consumption" labelling to sidestep medicines regulation. Independent testing of grey-market peptide vials has repeatedly found inaccurate dosing and contamination. There is no route to verifying what is actually in a vial bought this way.

        In the consult

        ## How to handle it clinically
        Ask broadly, without judgment"There's a lot of peptide content on social media at the moment - is that something you've looked into or tried?" This wave reaches a general wellness audience, not only competitive lifters - ask across a wide range of patients.

        Distinguish the prescribed from the self-sourcedConfirm whether a GLP-1 agonist is genuinely pharmacy-dispensed on prescription, or sourced through an online "peptide clinic" or overseas seller with limited oversight - the risk profile differs substantially even for the nominally same drug.

        Think of it as a causeConsider undisclosed peptide use behind: unexplained injection-site reactions or abscess, new or changing pigmented lesions in someone using a tanning peptide, unexpected glycaemic or weight change without a documented prescription, or fluid retention and unexplained soft-tissue growth suggesting a secretagogue.

        Harm reductionFor patients who will continue regardless: encourage sourcing from regulated pharmacy channels wherever a legitimate option exists, discuss injection technique and site rotation, and flag melanoma surveillance for anyone using melanotan II.
