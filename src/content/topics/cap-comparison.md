      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **CRP-guided prescribing:** NICE is distinctive in backing point-of-care CRP to decide on antibiotics in primary care.
        **Amoxicillin first:** all four anchor mild CAP on amoxicillin, with doxycycline a common alternative.
        **Short courses:** around 5 days suffices when the patient is improving - extended only for slow response.
