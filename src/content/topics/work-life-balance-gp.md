## Overview


General practitioner burnout has emerged as a significant public health concern across high-income healthcare systems. International data indicate rising rates of psychological distress, with burnout prevalence varying considerably between countries - a pattern that implicates structural, rather than individual, determinants. This review examines work-life balance in general practice by comparing workload metrics, working hours, consultation patterns, and burnout rates across jurisdictions, and identifies organisational factors that predict sustainable practice.
