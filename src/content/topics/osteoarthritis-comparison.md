      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **Paracetamol downgraded:** NICE 2022 went furthest, advising against routine paracetamol; others allow limited short-term use.
        **Exercise is the core:** all four put therapeutic exercise, education and weight loss ahead of any drug.
        **Opioids out:** every guideline discourages opioids in OA - NICE most emphatically.
