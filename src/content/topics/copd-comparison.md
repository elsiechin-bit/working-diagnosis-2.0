      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **Assessment:** GOLD's ABE grouping is the international anchor; NICE deliberately stays less category-driven.
        **ICS use:** all reserve inhaled steroids for exacerbators with higher eosinophils, but the thresholds and emphasis differ.
        **Foundations:** smoking cessation, vaccination and pulmonary rehab are universal - the real gap is access to rehabilitation.
