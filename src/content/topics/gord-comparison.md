      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **Triple to quadruple:** international guidance (Maastricht VI) is shifting toward bismuth quadruple therapy as clarithromycin resistance rises.
        **Test-and-treat:** for dyspepsia without alarms, testing for H. pylori and treating is preferred over open-ended PPIs.
        **Deprescribe PPIs:** every guideline now emphasises the lowest effective dose, step-down and review of long-term PPI use.
