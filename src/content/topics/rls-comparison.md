    ### Where they actually differ
    
      **Diagnostic approach:** All use IRLSSG criteria, but primary care recognition varies. UK emphasises symptom severity scoring (IRLS scale); international guidelines advocate questionnaire-based case finding.
      **First-line pharmacotherapy:** Dopamine agonists (pramipexole, ropinirole, rotigotine patch) dominate globally. NZ/AU favour pramipexole or ropinirole; UK often starts with levodopa (lower augmentation risk perceived); US guidelines increasingly favour dopamine agonists.
      **Augmentation prevention:** All recognise the risk of augmentation (symptom escalation with long-term dopamine agonist use). NZ/AU suggest monitoring; UK/International guideline emphasise dose minimisation and early combination therapy or rotation.
      **Iron supplementation:** All recommend checking ferritin. AU/International guidelines advocate iron replacement if ferritin <75 - 100 µg/L (more aggressive than NZ/UK).
      **Refractory disease:** Opioids (more accepted in US/UK for severe cases); gabapentin/pregabalin preferred in NZ/AU for opioid-sparing approach.
