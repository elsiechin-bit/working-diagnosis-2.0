#### PsoriasisChronic inflammatory skin disease; Th17/Th1 immune activation; 2-3% prevalence.
#### Plaque psoriasis (80%)- Well-demarcated erythematous plaques with silvery scale; extensor elbows, knees, scalp.#### Topical therapy- Corticosteroids (mild-moderate potency); vitamin D analogues (calcipotriol); calcineurin inhibitors.#### Systemic: Biologics- TNF inhibitors, IL-17 inhibitors (secukinumab), IL-23 inhibitors; 70-90% improvement.

      📚Key references▾

          #### Key guidelines & evidence
          
            - BAD (British Association of Dermatologists) Psoriasis Guidelines.
            - AAD (American Academy of Dermatology) Psoriasis Guidelines (updated regularly).
            - Topical corticosteroids first-line for localized disease.
            - TNF-inhibitors (etanercept, infliximab, adalimumab) for moderate-severe psoriasis.
            - IL-17 inhibitors (secukinumab, ixekizumab) showing superior efficacy in some trials.
            - IL-23 inhibitors (risankizumab) emerging as highly effective.
            - Cardiovascular and metabolic screening essential — psoriasis is systemic disease.
            - NZ Dermatology Society resources.
