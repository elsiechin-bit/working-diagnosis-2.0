      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **Funding gateways:** NZ's Special Authority for sacubitril/valsartan and the UK's specialist-initiation rules shape who gets ARNI and when - the clinical advice itself is closely aligned.
        **SGLT2i breadth:** the international guidelines (ESC 2023, AHA/ACC) extend SGLT2 inhibitors across the entire EF spectrum earliest; the others are catching up, especially in HFpEF.
        **Phenotyping:** NICE keeps a pragmatic reduced-vs-preserved split, while ESC, AHA/ACC and NHFA/CSANZ formally name the mildly-reduced (41–49%) band.
