      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **Permissive shift:** all four now frame MHT more positively for symptomatic women, moving past the post-WHI caution.
        **Route matters:** transdermal estradiol with micronised progesterone is the shared preference, avoiding oral VTE risk.
        **No time limit:** the "lowest dose, shortest time" rule has been replaced by individualised, benefit-led duration.
