      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **Reliever revolution:** all four have abandoned SABA-only for ICS-formoterol relievers - a shift underpinned by NZ-led trials.
        **Diagnosis:** the UK's 2024 algorithm leads with FeNO and eosinophils, where GINA and NZ/AU centre on spirometry and reversibility.
        **Severe asthma:** the science agrees on phenotype-guided biologics, but funding and access pathways differ between systems.
