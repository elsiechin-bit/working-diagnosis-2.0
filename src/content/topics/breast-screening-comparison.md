      ### Where they actually differ
  **Start age:** The USPSTF's 2024 reversal to recommending screening from age 40 (biennial) is the most significant recent divergence. UK still starts at 50 (with plans to extend to 47). NZ and AU start at 40–45.
  **Interval:** UK uses three-yearly mammography (longest interval); NZ, AU and the US recommend two-yearly for most ages.
  **Dense breast tissue:** US requires notification of dense breast density under federal law; NZ/AU/UK practice varies without a unified policy.
