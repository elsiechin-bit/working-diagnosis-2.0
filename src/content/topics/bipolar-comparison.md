      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **Valproate restriction:** the UK enforces a formal Pregnancy Prevention Programme; others strongly advise avoidance without the same regulatory framework.
        **Bipolar depression:** NICE specifically warns against antidepressant monotherapy; all converge on quetiapine/lurasidone/lithium.
        **Shared ground:** lithium remains first-line maintenance everywhere, with structured level and organ monitoring.
