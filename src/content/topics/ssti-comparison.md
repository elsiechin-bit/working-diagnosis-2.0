      ### Where they actually differ
      
        **Flucloxacillin holds:** NZ, Australia and the UK all anchor cellulitis treatment on flucloxacillin; the IDSA frames it as a penicillinase-resistant β-lactam.
        **MRSA cover:** more routinely empirical for purulent infection in US (IDSA) practice than in NZ/AU/UK, where it is risk-guided.
        **NZ context:** the high burden of serious skin infection and rheumatic-fever risk in Māori and Pacific communities shapes NZ thresholds.
