#### Reproductive Manifestations
          
            - Oligomenorrhoea or amenorrhoea
            - Infertility or difficulty conceiving
            - Increased pregnancy loss
          
          #### Androgenic Features
          
            - Hirsutism or acne
            - Male-pattern baldness
            - Elevated testosterone or androstenedione
          
          #### Metabolic Complications
          
            - Insulin resistance in 50–70%
            - Obesity and weight gain
            - Increased type 2 diabetes risk

          Management Strategy
          ^

          #### Lifestyle
          
            - Weight loss if overweight (5–10% improves ovulation and insulin resistance)
            - Exercise and dietary management
          
          #### Pharmacotherapy
          
            - Combined hormonal contraceptives for menstrual regulation and hyperandrogenism
            - Metformin for insulin resistance
            - Spironolactone for hirsutism
            - Clomiphene citrate or letrozole for ovulation induction
