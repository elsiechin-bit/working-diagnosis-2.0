## Overview


Materia Medica
    An interactive compendium of medicinal plants & their evidence base

    Active phytochemicals, mechanisms, clinical evidence, safety profiles, and known interactions for commonly-used medicinal herbs.

      **Educational reference only.** Information here is summarised from secondary sources (Cochrane reviews, NCCIH, EMA herbal monographs, peer-reviewed reviews). It is not medical advice. Many herbal products are unregulated; potency varies between preparations. Always consult a qualified clinician before use, particularly if pregnant, on prescription medication, or with chronic illness.

      **Evidence tiers:**
      Strong - multiple positive RCTs / Cochrane support
      Moderate - some positive trials, mixed
      Limited - preliminary or small studies
      Insufficient - traditional use only
      Negative - trials have not supported use
