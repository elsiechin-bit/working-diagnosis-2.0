      ### Where they actually differ
  **No jurisdiction recommends population screening:** All use individual informed discussion rather than an organised recall programme, though the EAU and some international bodies are moving toward risk-stratified organised detection.
  **Age and risk thresholds diverge:** NZ and UK focus on the 50–69 range; ACS recommends starting the discussion at 40 for very high-risk men; USPSTF recommends considering it from 55 unless high-risk.
  **PSA threshold for referral:** NZ and AU use ~4 ng/mL; UK uses 3 ng/mL for age 50–69; EAU uses age-specific thresholds (e.g. 2.5 ng/mL at 50–59).
