#### What to record
          
            - Lesion types (comedones vs inflammatory vs nodules/cysts), distribution (face, chest, back) and severity
            - Scarring and post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation - a key driver to treat aggressively
            - Psychological impact - acne is strongly associated with low mood and social withdrawal; ask directly
            - Current and prior treatments, adherence and duration (most need 8-12 weeks to judge)
          
          #### Consider an endocrine cause
          
            In women with acne plus irregular periods, hirsutism or signs of hyperandrogenism, consider **PCOS** and investigate accordingly
            - Sudden severe acne, virilisation or rapid onset warrants work-up for an androgen-secreting cause

        🧴Topical therapy▾
        
          #### Building blocks
          
            **Topical retinoids** (adapalene, tretinoin) - the backbone; comedolytic and anti-inflammatory. Warn about initial irritation and photosensitivity; start alternate nights.
            **Benzoyl peroxide** - antimicrobial that does not drive resistance; reduces C. acnes. Warn it bleaches fabric.
            **Topical antibiotics** (clindamycin) - only ever combined with benzoyl peroxide or a retinoid to limit resistance, never as monotherapy.
            **Azelaic acid** - useful in pigmentation and in pregnancy.
          
          Give it timeSet expectations: meaningful improvement takes 8-12 weeks. Most "treatment failures" are stopping too early or applying too little.

        💊Oral therapy▾
        
          #### Oral antibiotics
          
            **Doxycycline** (or lymecycline) for moderate-to-severe inflammatory acne, always with a topical retinoid and benzoyl peroxide
            - Limit courses to around 3 months to curb antimicrobial resistance; continue topicals as maintenance
            - Avoid tetracyclines in pregnancy and under 12 years
          
          #### Hormonal therapy (women)
          
            **Combined oral contraceptives** reduce androgen-driven acne and are a good option when contraception is also wanted
            **Spironolactone** is a useful anti-androgen for persistent adult female acne

        ⚠️Isotretinoin▾
        
          #### When
          
            - Severe nodulocystic acne, scarring acne, or moderate acne unresponsive to adequate courses of oral antibiotics plus topicals
            - Significant psychological impact
          
          #### Key safety points
          
            **Highly teratogenic** - strict pregnancy prevention is mandatory for people who can become pregnant (effective contraception, pregnancy testing before, during and after)
            - Monitor lipids and liver function; counsel on dryness, photosensitivity and mucocutaneous effects
            - Discuss mood - assess and safety-net for mood change, and review
          
          Pregnancy and isotretinoinIsotretinoin causes major congenital malformations. It must never be used in pregnancy, and robust contraception with a pregnancy-prevention programme is required throughout and for one month after stopping.

      Acne fulminans / very severe flaresSudden severe ulcerating or systemic acne (fever, arthralgia) is a dermatological urgency - refer; isotretinoin started too fast can precipitate a flare, so specialists often cover with corticosteroid.
