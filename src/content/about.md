Working Diagnosis is a clinical reference website for general practitioners and primary care clinicians in Aotearoa New Zealand.

## Purpose

To organise evidence-based medicine into fast, scannable reference surfaces that GPs can glance at between patients. Topics include guideline comparisons, clinical decision tools, drug references, patient education, and essays from practice.

## Design principles

- **NZ-first sourcing** - using BPAC, HealthPathways, NZF, PHARMAC, and MoH as primary sources
- **Clinical precision** - all scoring thresholds and clinical content verified against primary sources before implementation
- **Structural gaps** - filling the gaps that international platforms cannot (PHARMAC/Special Authority, ACC vs medical, primary-secondary interface)
- **Equity lens** - Māori and Pasifika health outcomes, te whare tapa whā, indigenous data, PHARMAC access disparities

## Disclaimer

This site is experimental reference material for qualified clinicians. Always apply clinical judgment; verify against current guidelines, local formularies, and individual patient context.
